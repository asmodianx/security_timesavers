6. Eradicate & Recover

  
Remove the cause, restore normal operation, and confirm each step worked.

  
6.1 Eradication actions

    
Remove the cause: malware, persistence, compromised credentials, exploited vulnerabilities.

    

  

  
6.2 Recovery actions

    
Restore systems to normal operation and confirm they work, e.g. rebuild, restore from backup, re-enable access, heightened monitoring.
